## 3. Permitted Informational Uses

You may use the word mark "Edufeedia" without prior written permission solely for truthful, non-confusing, descriptive, and non-commercial purposes:
- To state that your software is an extension, adapter, plugin, or client for Edufeedia (e.g. "CBSE Curriculum Adapter for Edufeedia").
- To accurately refer to the Edufeedia project in technical articles, educational research, academic citations, or community discussions.
- To link to the official repository at https://github.com/rehan0018/Edufeedia.

## 4. Prohibited Uses

Without prior express written permission from Rehan Shaikh, you may NOT:
1. **Name Products or Services**: Use "Edufeedia" (or confusingly similar variations) as the primary name of your software, company, commercial service, domain name, or application.
2. **Implied Endorsement**: Use Edufeedia trademarks in any manner that suggests official endorsement, sponsorship, partnership, or affiliation by Rehan Shaikh or Edufeedia.
3. **Rebranding or White-labeling**: Distribute modified or unmodified versions of the software under the Edufeedia brand while offering a separate commercial service.
4. **Altered Logos**: Modify, distort, recolor, or combine Edufeedia logos with third-party marks.
